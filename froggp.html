HOW TO RACE
Your finger is the car. Sort of.

1. Hold the flashing dot on the start line.
2. On GO, trace the circuit — every lap — as fast as you can.
3. Your frog then drives your exact line at your exact speed.

Scrape a wall and the frog scrapes it too, and slows way down. Cutting across the grass doesn’t count as a lap. The stopwatch doesn’t care about your excuses.

The perfect racing line, drawn fast, wins. That’s the whole sport.
